About Me

我是面向具身智能、家庭服务机器人与硬件快速原型的全栈机器人开发者，具备从方案设计、硬件原型、底层通信、感知导航、遥操作数据采集到 VLA 模型适配的端到端开发经验。目前在原力无限（INFIFORCE）深圳研究院参与家庭服务机器人研发，推进本体搭建、遥操作系统、数据采集链路与 VLA 训练相关工作。

本科就读于北师香港浸会大学数据科学专业，已录取澳门大学机器人与自主系统（RAS）理学硕士（2026 年秋入学）。曾从 0 到 1 创立 35 人高校机器人实验室 Navigator Robotics Lab，带队获 RoboMaster 工程挑战赛辽宁站第一名；在 ICML 2025 以 Poster 形式展示上下文学习相关研究工作。

我长期以工程视角做 AI：关注 Sim-to-Real、动态环境 SLAM、长程任务智能体与跨本体机器人操作。计划自 2028 年秋申请机器人/AI 方向全奖 Ph.D.，继续探索鲁棒感知与灵巧操作。

此外，我具备硬件黑客松场景下的快速原型与 Demo 交付能力（需求拆解、跨栈协作、现场联调）。报名材料见 黑客松专用简历。

I am a full-stack robotics developer focused on embodied AI, home service robots, and rapid hardware prototyping, with end-to-end experience from system design and hardware bring-up to perception, navigation, teleoperation-based data collection, and VLA model adaptation. I currently work at the INFIFORCE Shenzhen Research Institute on home service robot development, teleoperation systems, data pipelines, and VLA training.

I hold a B.Sc. in Data Science from Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University and have been admitted to the M.Sc. in Robotics and Autonomous Systems (RAS) at the University of Macau (Fall 2026). I founded the 35-member Navigator Robotics Lab and led the team to 1st Place in the RoboMaster Regional Engineer Challenge; I also presented work at ICML 2025 (Poster).

I approach AI from an engineering perspective—Sim-to-Real, SLAM in dynamic environments, long-horizon agents, and cross-embodiment manipulation. I plan to pursue a fully funded Ph.D. in Robotics/AI starting Fall 2028, focusing on robust perception and dexterous manipulation.

Demonstration Selection for In-Context Learning via Reinforcement Learning

Paper | ICML Official | Poster

Authors: Xubin Wang, Jianfei Wu, Yichen Yuan, Deyu Cai, Mingzhe Li, Weijia Jia
Published: December 5, 2024
Conference: ICML 2025 (International Conference on Machine Learning 2025)
Preprint: arXiv preprint arXiv:2412.03966
Abstract:
Diversity in demonstration selection is critical for enhancing model generalization by enabling broader coverage of structures and concepts. Constructing appropriate demonstration sets remains a key research challenge.
This paper introduces the Relevance-Diversity Enhanced Selection (RDES), an innovative approach that leverages reinforcement learning (RL) frameworks to optimize the selection of diverse reference demonstrations for tasks amenable to in-context learning (ICL), particularly text classification and reasoning, in few-shot prompting scenarios.
