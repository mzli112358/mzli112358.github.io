涂鸦机器人

Graffiti Robot

一个能走到墙前的身体，拿着喷头，按颜色一层一层把图案喷上去。层的顺序是画面的一部分，先喷和后喷盖住的关系不一样。这页只定了要用来做它的身体，样机还没有。

A body that can walk up to a wall and lay color down one layer at a time. Layer order is part of the picture: what goes on first changes what the next layer covers. This page only fixes the body I want to use. There is no prototype yet.

Already in the kit

  • 地瓜 RDK，做端侧视觉和运动这边的计算。

  • A depth camera, for wall distance and for what is already on the wall.

  • A sprayer mount, 3D-printed on site if none is ready.

它要做成的事

The Job

大面上的涂装不要再靠好几台单色设备轮流上。一台机器按图层走：这一层是什么颜色、喷在哪、排在第几步。视觉闭环看的是墙上的结果，不是只看手臂有没有走完轨迹。墙距一旦漂，喷幅和臂的路径会一起漂。

Large-surface color should not need several single-color machines taking turns. One machine walks the layers: which color, where it goes, and which step it is. The visual loop judges the wall, not only whether the arm finished its path. If the wall distance drifts, spray width and the arm path drift with it.

模块，按这个顺序

Modules, in this order

  • 身体开到墙前，定位，把墙和工作空间留在地图里。
  • 稳住相对墙的距离，给喷头留出间隙。
  • 在这个间隙里规划臂，并让身体和臂一起动。
  • 把一张图拆成按颜色、按顺序的层，再按层喷；看着墙面结果回头修。
  • Drive up to the wall, localize, and keep the wall and the workspace on the map.
  • Hold the distance to the wall so the sprayer has a stable gap.
  • Plan the arm inside that gap, and move the body and the arm together.
  • Split one image into ordered color layers, spray them in order, and correct from the wall.

现在还没有的

What Does Not Exist Yet

  • 没有样机，没有代码仓库，RDK 上也还没有跑通过。
  • 实体喷头、换色方式和墙面材料仍待验证。仿真的小鸭图案用于演示。
  • 人形和「狗加臂」哪一条先上，等看到当天的机器再定。狗这一条里，维他动力优先于宇树。
  • No prototype, no repository, and nothing running on an RDK yet.
  • The real sprayer, color changes, and wall material still need validation. The duck mural is a simulation example.
  • Humanoid versus dog-plus-arm waits until the machines on site are in hand. If it is a dog, Vita Dynamics comes before Unitree.
