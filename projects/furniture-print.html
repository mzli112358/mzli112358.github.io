室内米级协作臂 3D 打印平台

Indoor Meter-Scale Collaborative-Arm 3D Printing

用移动协作臂在室内完成家具尺度（约 0.3–3 m）连续增材制造：定位、运动、切片与挤出控制组成可扩展的自主打印系统。

A mobile collaborative-arm platform for indoor furniture-scale (about 0.3–3 m) additive manufacturing—localization, motion, slicing, and extrusion as one extensible autonomous print system.

2026年7月 — 2027年1月（预计） · 李明哲

Jul 2026 – Jan 2027 (expected) · Mingzhe Li

为什么做：尺度空白

Why: a Scale Gap

消费级桌面 3D 打印（如拓竹、创想三维）成型尺寸多在约 30 cm 以内；更大尺寸往往跳到昂贵工业/特种材料增材，或十米级建筑水泥打印。约 0.3–3 m 的家具与室内物件尺度，在尺寸、材料、喷头与成本上都不匹配——这是一个尴尬但可挑战的区间。

Consumer desktop printers (e.g., Bambu Lab, Creality) are typically within ~30 cm. Larger scales jump to costly industrial/specialty-material AM or building-scale cement printers. The ~0.3–3 m furniture/indoor band is awkward in size, materials, nozzles, and cost—and worth challenging.

目标

Goal

搭建可在室内移动作业的打印平台，在约百日量级周期内完成系统开发，并实现稳定打印家具尺度物件。这是一个偏系统工程的挑战（类似用乐高搭椅子/赛车那种“能跑通全链路”的问题），而不是单点算法论文。

Build a mobile indoor printing platform, develop the full stack on a roughly 100-day timeline, and achieve stable furniture-scale prints. It is a systems-engineering challenge (full-stack closure), not a single-algorithm paper.

系统构成

System

移动操作打印单元

Mobile manipulative print unit

拟采用高擎科技协作臂 + 移动底盘（松灵 / 思岚或自研），构成可直行、可转向的室内打印机体。

Planned Gaoqing Tech collaborative arm on a mobile base (AgileX / Slamtec or self-built) for indoor traverse and print motion.

高精度室内定位融合

Fused indoor localization

UWB、视觉标记、激光雷达与轮式里程计各有瞬时/累积误差；需要融合方案支撑长时连续打印。

UWB, visual markers, LiDAR, and wheel odometry each have transient/drift errors; fusion is required for long-horizon continuous printing.
