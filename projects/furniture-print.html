室内米级协作臂 3D 打印平台

Indoor Meter-Scale Collaborative-Arm 3D Printing

原构想是用移动协作臂在室内完成家具尺度（约 0.3–3 m）连续增材制造，将定位、运动、切片与挤出控制整合为自主打印系统。项目现已暂停。

The original concept was a mobile collaborative-arm platform for indoor furniture-scale (about 0.3–3 m) additive manufacturing, integrating localization, motion, slicing, and extrusion. The project is now on hold.

原始动机：尺度空白

Original Motivation: a Scale Gap

消费级桌面 3D 打印（如拓竹、创想三维）成型尺寸多在约 30 cm 以内；更大尺寸往往跳到昂贵工业/特种材料增材，或十米级建筑水泥打印。约 0.3–3 m 的家具与室内物件尺度，在尺寸、材料、喷头与成本上都不匹配——这是一个尴尬但可挑战的区间。

Consumer desktop printers (e.g., Bambu Lab, Creality) are typically within ~30 cm. Larger scales jump to costly industrial/specialty-material AM or building-scale cement printers. The ~0.3–3 m furniture/indoor band is awkward in size, materials, nozzles, and cost—and worth challenging.

原计划目标

Original Goal

原计划搭建可在室内移动作业的打印平台，在约百日量级周期内完成系统开发，并实现家具尺度物件的稳定打印。项目以系统集成为主；这些目标尚未完成，目前已暂停。

The original goal was to build a mobile indoor printing platform, develop the full stack on a roughly 100-day timeline, and achieve stable furniture-scale prints. These systems-integration goals were not completed; the project is now on hold.

原计划系统构成

Planned System

移动操作打印单元

Mobile manipulative print unit

原方案设想采用协作臂 + 移动底盘，构成可直行、可转向的室内打印机体；平台选型与系统集成尚未完成。

The planned architecture combined a collaborative arm with a mobile base for indoor traverse and print motion; platform selection and system integration were not completed.

高精度室内定位融合

Fused indoor localization

原方案考虑融合 UWB、视觉标记、激光雷达与轮式里程计，以处理瞬时与累积误差，支撑长时连续打印；尚未完成系统验证。

The planned approach considered fusing UWB, visual markers, LiDAR, and wheel odometry to address transient errors and drift during continuous printing; system validation was not completed.
